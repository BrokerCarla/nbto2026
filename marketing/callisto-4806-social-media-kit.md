## ⚠️ Before posting: fix these first

1. **"Delivered bare" vs. "semi-furnished".** The intro says *semi-furnished*, but the spec list says *delivered bare*. Choose one wording and use it everywhere. Suggested: **"Delivered semi-furnished: brand-new induction stove, 2-door refrigerator, and inverter ACs installed."**
2. **The virtual staging disclaimer has to go on every post, reel, and ad.** Put it in the caption, and also as text on the image or video itself, because people often see renders shared without the caption. Suggested overlay: `VIRTUALLY STAGED · For illustration only`.
3. **The claim that it's "below a standard 3BR in the same building"** is the strongest hook you have, and buyers will test it. Keep one or two recent comparable asking prices on hand so you can back it up in DMs.
4. **Price math is correct:** ₱36,000,000 ÷ 118 sqm = ₱305,085/sqm. The cut is ₱9M, or **20% off**.
5. Add your **PRC/REB license no.** (and DHSUD registration if applicable) to posts and ads, as RESA rules require.
