## 2. Budget split (₱3,000 / 15 days)

₱200/day is small. Spread across 4 platforms, it would be too thin for any of them to deliver. Put most of it where real-estate leads actually come from.

| Platform | Budget | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook + Instagram** (one Meta campaign) | **₱2,400** (₱160/day × 15) | Ads Manager → **Messages** objective (Messenger + WhatsApp + IG Direct), Advantage+ placements |
| **TikTok** | **₱600** | TikTok **Promote** on the 20s video. Run it as a 5-day burst (~₱120/day) on days 1–5, when the post is fresh |
| **YouTube Shorts** | **₱0 (organic)** | Post the same 20s video as a Short with the SEO title below. ₱300 on Google Ads is too little for it to learn and optimize |

**Why one Meta campaign:** a single ad set feeds both FB and IG. Meta optimizes across the two automatically, so you don't split ₱2,400 into two weak boosts.

## 3. Meta setup (FB + IG)

- **Use Ads Manager, not the "Boost" button.** Boost gives fewer controls and weaker objectives.
- **Objective:** Engagement → **Messages**. You want conversations, not likes.
- **Special Ad Category:** If Meta asks, declare **Housing**. Skipping a required declaration is a common rejection reason. Housing limits targeting options, which is fine here.
- **Location:** Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Rizal, Bulacan. If you want OFW buyers, add a 2nd ad set later with *extra* budget. Don't split ₱160/day.
- **Age:** 28–65+ (Housing may lock this to 18–65+; that's OK)
- **Audience:** Advantage+ / broad. Let the creative find buyers.
- **Ads (2 in the same ad set; Meta will favor the winner):**
  - **Ad A:** 20s vertical video
  - **Ad B:** photo carousel (order above)
- **Message template greeting:** "Hi! Thanks for your interest in Callisto Tower 2 Unit 4806 🏙️ Would you like the full photo set, floor plan, or to schedule a viewing?"
  Quick replies: `Send full photos` · `Schedule viewing` · `Payment terms`

### Rejection-proofing checklist
- [ ] Real photos/video only, with no AI, renders, or staging
- [ ] No "before/after" or "Vision vs Reality" framing
- [ ] Minimal text on images (price + location only)
- [ ] No sensational claims ("guaranteed ROI", "best investment")
- [ ] Caption features match what the photos show (stove type, semi-furnished vs. bare)
- [ ] Name + PRC REB license no. in caption
- [ ] Housing special ad category declared if prompted
- [ ] Your Facebook Page has your contact info and agent name filled in
